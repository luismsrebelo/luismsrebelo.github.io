I'm **Luís Rebelo**, a PhD student in Biomedical Engineering and Biophysics at the Faculty of Sciences, University of Lisbon (FCUL), and a software engineer with over 12 years of professional experience. My research focuses on applying **artificial intelligence and digital health technologies to real-world healthcare challenges**.

I founded Macroanalytica, where I build software and IT solutions. Before that, I spent more than eight years at Vodafone Portugal as a Product Owner and Content Management Specialist, and started my career as a full-stack engineer at Mindshaker. I'm also a certified trainer (CCP) and teach Information and Communication Technologies in vocational education.

## Research interests

- Artificial intelligence and machine learning in healthcare
- Digital health
- Digital twins and computer simulation
- Edge computing (RISC-V)
- Biomedical data analysis
